Usually I barely manage to finish up my [weekly beats track](https://weeklybeats.com/jimmac) on a Sunday night. This week I've somehow had some extra time to sink into polishing an actual full track on the [Dirtywave M8](https://dirtywave.com). Built around the bassline where I've mimicked the approach [used on the Analog 4](/posts/innit/) of fading in a modulated filter and volume pulse over time using slight different tools (the M8 has 4 LFOs and ability to modulate a modulator).
